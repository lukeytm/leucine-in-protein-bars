High-leucine protein bars without collagen

Leucent 3.0 (3.0 g leucine, in pre-production and not on sale) and EVOBAR (3.0–3.3 g, on sale) are the high-leucine bars we track that use no collagen. Quest also has none, at about 1.9–2.2 g. Collagen is only about 3% leucine against about 10–11% for whey, so a collagen-heavy bar can show a big protein number with much less leucine.

Why it matters

We list collagen in Pursuit, David, Grenade Carb Killa, Barebells and Built Puff, and in some Myprotein Layered flavours as collagen or gelatin. Check the ingredients for collagen, collagen hydrolysate, hydrolysed collagen peptides or gelatin, because formulas vary by flavour.

Common questions

What's the best protein bar without collagen?

Look for about 3 g of leucine and 20 g+ of protein with no collagen. Leucent 3.0 was designed around that but isn't on sale; EVOBAR is on sale and says it uses no collagen.

Why avoid collagen in a protein bar?

Collagen is about 3% leucine and isn't a complete protein, so it adds protein grams that do little for muscle protein synthesis.

Which popular protein bars contain collagen?

Pursuit, David, Grenade Carb Killa, Barebells and Built Puff, plus some Myprotein Layered flavours. Check the label of the flavour you buy.
