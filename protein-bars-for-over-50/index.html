High-leucine protein bars for over 50s

After 50, muscle responds less to protein, so look for a bar with about 3 g of leucine and at least 20 g of complete protein. Leucent 3.0 was designed around exactly that (3.0 g leucine, 21 g whey and milk protein, no collagen), but it is in pre-production and not on sale. On sale now, EVOBAR (3.0–3.3 g leucine, 20–23 g protein) and Pursuit (about 3.5 g by the brand's estimate, 26.5 g protein, includes collagen) meet both.

Why it matters

Research on older men found about 0.4 g of protein per kg of body weight per meal was needed to maximally stimulate muscle protein synthesis: about 28 g for a 70 kg person. That's why the leucine target rises to about 3 g per feeding.

Common questions

What's the best protein bar for a lifter over 50?

One with about 3 g of leucine and at least 20 g of complete protein. Leucent 3.0 meets both but isn't on sale yet; EVOBAR and Pursuit meet both and are on sale.

How much protein should I eat per meal after 50?

About 0.4 g per kg of body weight per meal, or roughly 28 g for a 70 kg person, based on research in older men.

Is a protein bar enough on its own?

No. Strength training and enough protein across the day matter more than any one bar. A high-leucine bar helps most where a meal or snack would otherwise fall short.
