How much leucine is in a Quest bar?

We estimate 1.9–2.2 g of leucine in a Quest Protein Bar. Quest doesn't list it, so the figure comes from its 21 g of milk and whey protein isolate, which carry roughly 9–10.5% leucine. That's close to, but short of, the 2.5–3 g per meal research suggests, and about 1 g short of the 3 g suggested for lifters over 50.
