Common questions

Which protein bar has the most leucine?

Of the 16 bars here, Pursuit's brand estimate (about 3.5 g) is the highest figure for a bar on sale, followed by EVOBAR (3.0–3.3 g), Leucent 3.0 (3.0 g, not yet on sale) and Myprotein Loaded EAA (3.0 g).

Is there a protein bar with 3 g of leucine?

Yes. Leucent 3.0, EVOBAR, Myprotein Loaded EAA and Pursuit all reach about 3 g of leucine per bar. Leucent 3.0 is in pre-production and not on sale yet.

How much leucine is in most protein bars?

About 1.5–2.2 g per bar for popular bars such as Quest, Barebells, Grenade and ONE. That is below the roughly 2.5–3 g per meal that research links to maximal muscle protein synthesis.

Which high-leucine protein bars have no collagen?

Leucent 3.0 and EVOBAR. Collagen adds protein grams but is only about 3% leucine, so collagen-heavy bars score lower on leucine than their protein figure suggests.

How the estimates work

Where a brand doesn't publish leucine, we estimate it from protein grams and sources. Typical leucine shares: whey isolate 10–11%, milk protein and casein 9–10%, egg white 8.5%, soy isolate 8%, collagen or gelatin 3%. These are estimates, not lab results. If a brand publishes an amino acid profile, that number beats ours. Figures checked October and November 2026; formulas vary by flavour and country.
