Why it matters

Research links about 2.5–3 g of leucine per meal to maximal muscle protein synthesis, rising to about 3 g for adults over 50. A bar has to add free leucine, or carry well over 25 g of dairy protein, to get there.

Common questions

Is there a protein bar with 3 g of leucine?

Yes. Leucent 3.0, EVOBAR, Myprotein Loaded EAA and Pursuit all reach about 3 g, and ADDRA Labs is close. Leucent 3.0 is in pre-production and not on sale yet.

Which protein bar has the most leucine?

Of the bars we track, Pursuit's estimate of about 3.5 g is the highest figure for a bar on sale. It is the brand's own estimate, not a lab result.

Why do so few protein bars reach 3 g of leucine?

Whey and milk protein are about 9–11% leucine, so 21 g of protein gives roughly 2 g. Reaching 3 g needs added L-leucine or a much bigger bar.
